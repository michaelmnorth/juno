Juno

A personal assistant app, used only by Michael North.

Juno is a private, single-user app. It helps Michael keep track of his own email, calendar and reminders. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It asks Google for access to Michael's own Gmail and Google Calendar, to read, label and draft mail and to prepare calendar events. It never sends email, invites anyone or shares data on its own; anything outward needs Michael's approval first.
